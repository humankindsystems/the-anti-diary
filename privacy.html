Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HumanKind Systems LLC (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the anti-diary mobile application (“App” or “Service”).

the anti-diary is designed to give users a private place to write, release, and optionally keep personal entries without requiring an account.

We do not require you to create an account to use the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be stored, processed, or received when you use the anti-diary.

The App is designed so that HumanKind Systems LLC does not operate a server that receives or stores the private content you write in the App.

Certain App content is stored locally on your device. Saved Entries may also be stored or synchronized through Apple services such as iCloud and CloudKit, depending on your device, Apple ID, iCloud availability, and system settings.

2. Private Content

HumanKind Systems LLC does not receive your journal entries, unsent messages, unsent email drafts, or voicemail recordings on our own servers.

Depending on the feature you use, the App may store information such as:

  • Written entries
  • Temporary writing drafts
  • Saved Entries
  • Temporary unsent message drafts
  • Temporary unsent email drafts
  • Temporary voicemail recordings
  • App preferences and settings
  • Saved Entries lock preferences
  • Purchase entitlement status

Temporary drafts and recordings are used to provide the App's features and recovery behavior. HumanKind Systems LLC does not use the content of your private writing or recordings for advertising, profiling, marketing, or behavioral tracking.

3. No Account Required

The App does not currently require you to create an account.

Because there is no HumanKind Systems account system for the App, we do not collect account usernames, account passwords, or profile information through an Anti-Diary account.

4. Local Storage and iCloud

Temporary drafts, preferences, and other App state may be stored locally on your device.

Saved Entries may also use Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services to support storage and synchronization across compatible devices when those services are available and enabled.

Data stored in a user's private CloudKit database is managed through the user's Apple iCloud account. HumanKind Systems LLC does not operate its own server for storing or reading your Saved Entry content.

Apple may also include certain App data in device or iCloud backups depending on your Apple settings. HumanKind Systems LLC does not control Apple’s iCloud, CloudKit, or backup systems.

You can review and manage relevant iCloud and backup settings through your device settings.

5. Saved Entries Security

The App allows you to protect Saved Entries using available device security or a custom PIN or password.

Custom PINs and passwords are not stored by the App in readable form. The App stores protected verification information using Apple Keychain services on the device.

If you use device authentication, Apple system features such as Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID where supported, or the device passcode may be used to confirm device ownership. HumanKind Systems LLC does not receive your biometric data.

6. Microphone Access

The App may request microphone access if you choose to use the voicemail-style feature in the Unsent Pack.

Microphone access is used to allow you to record an unsent voicemail within the App.

HumanKind Systems LLC does not receive, store, or listen to your voicemail recordings on our servers.

Voicemail recordings created for this feature are temporary and are removed as part of the App's send, reset, or cleanup flow.

You can disable microphone access at any time in your device settings.

7. In-App Purchases and RevenueCat

The App may offer non-subscription, one-time in-app purchases, including optional feature upgrades.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. HumanKind Systems LLC does not receive or store your payment card number, bank account information, or other direct payment credentials.

We use RevenueCat, a third-party purchase-management service, to verify purchases and determine whether purchased features should be unlocked.

In connection with this service, RevenueCat may process purchase-history, transaction, entitlement, receipt, and limited technical information needed to provide purchase functionality and related purchase analytics.

This may include information such as an Apple purchase receipt, purchase status, entitlement information, device type, operating system, and related transaction information.

RevenueCat does not receive the private journal entries, unsent messages, unsent email content, or voicemail content you create in the App through our RevenueCat integration.

RevenueCat processes information according to its own privacy practices.

8. Information We May Receive From You

We may receive limited personal information if you contact HumanKind Systems LLC directly for support, feedback, or another business-related reason.

This may include:

  • Your email address
  • Your name, if you choose to provide it
  • The content of your support request
  • Device or iOS information you choose to provide
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in your communication

We use this information to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, provide support, and maintain appropriate business records.

9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current version of the App does not use advertising networks or third-party behavioral tracking tools.

We do not use your private writing, unsent communications, or voicemail content for advertising, behavioral tracking, profiling, or marketing.

RevenueCat provides purchase-related reporting and analytics associated with in-app purchase functionality. This is described in the In-App Purchases and RevenueCat section above.

The current version of the App does not use a separate third-party general usage analytics SDK or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

If we add advertising, additional analytics, crash reporting, new cloud services, account features, or similar tools in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures as needed.

10. Apple Services

The App relies on certain Apple technologies and services, which may include:

  • App Store and StoreKit for in-app purchases
  • iCloud and CloudKit for eligible Saved Entry storage and synchronization
  • Keychain for protected credential verification information
  • Local Authentication for Face ID, Touch ID, or device authentication
  • Apple device backup services, depending on your settings

Apple's handling of information through these services is governed by Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

When you visit those web pages, the hosting provider may process basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and access logs according to its own policies.
